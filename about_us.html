WHO WE ARE

The Saasify Journey

When I founded Saasify Solutions in 2018, my vision was simple yet ambitious — to empower enterprises to manage, automate, and secure their IT landscapes with solutions that are innovative, reliable, and scalable. What began as a small but determined operation in Chennai has grown into a thriving organization recognized for delivering value, trust, and excellence to customers across industries.

Over the years, we expanded through new branches, strategic alliances, and refined service offerings to meet evolving market needs. In 2025, we launched our Bangalore branch, strengthening our presence in one of India’s most dynamic tech hubs, while strategically consolidating operations in Hyderabad to sharpen our focus on high-growth markets.

A defining milestone came in 2024, when we partnered with ManageEngine, enabling us to deliver enterprise-grade implementations across ITSM, ITOM, UEM, IAM, and Security. This strengthened our portfolio and empowered us to serve complex enterprise IT requirements with scalable, future-ready solutions.

Today, we proudly serve 150+ customers and have successfully delivered 100+ implementations across industries and geographies. Our journey is driven by a customer-first mindset, innovation, and a dedicated team committed to building lasting partnerships.

Our Vision

To be the trusted technology partner and advisor for enterprises, delivering innovative, integrated solutions that manage, automate, and secure organizational IT. We strive to be the partner of choice for businesses worldwide in their digital transformation journey, driving innovation, efficiency, and sustainable growth.

Our Mission

To empower enterprises in achieving their digital ambitions by delivering end-to-end technology solutions — from strategic consulting and meticulous planning to seamless implementation and dedicated support. We aim to reduce IT complexities, enabling our clients to focus on their core business, while fostering continuous learning and growth for our team members.
